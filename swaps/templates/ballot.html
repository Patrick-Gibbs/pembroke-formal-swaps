Ballot groups

How group balloting works

Ballot as a group and you all get the same outcome: the group enters the ballot as one block, and for each formal you either all get places or none of you do. One person (the group leader) sets the ranking for everyone — on the My ranking page — and invites the others, who accept here.

  • You can be in one group per term. Accepting an invitation means your own ranking is set aside; the leader's ranking counts for you.
  • The group needs enough free seats for everyone at a formal, so big groups are harder to seat — groups are capped at {{ max_size }}.
  • Group placement doesn't jump any queue: in the random draw a group of three counts like three friends who happen to win or lose together.
  • You can leave (or the leader can disband) any time before the ballot runs. Cancelling after allocation is still individual.
