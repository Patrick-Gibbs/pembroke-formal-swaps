How the ballot works

This isn't first-come-first-served. When the ballot closes, an automated draw runs:

  • Rank only what you want. Order matters: everyone's 1st choice is considered before anyone's 2nd choice.
  • If a formal has more bidders than seats, winners are drawn at random (a recorded, re-runnable draw — no favouritism). If you miss out, you automatically drop to your next choice in the same pass.
  • Fairness first: nobody is given a second formal until everyone who can still be seated somewhere they ranked has one. You leave empty-handed only if everything you ranked filled up.
  • Ranking more formals never hurts your chances at your top choice — it only gives you fallbacks.
  • Groups (see Ballot groups) enter as one block with the leader's ranking and win or lose together.
