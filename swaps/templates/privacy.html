Privacy

How Pembroke Formal Swaps handles your personal data.

What we collect and why

  • Your name, Cambridge email and 4-digit PIN — to create your account, verify you're a Cambridge member, and log you in. The PIN is stored hashed (argon2id + a server-side pepper), never in plain text.
  • Dietary requirements — so host colleges can cater for you.
  • Your formal preferences, allocations, group memberships, slot alerts and reviews — to run the ballot and the site's features.
  • Basic security logs (login attempts by email/IP) — to rate-limit and protect accounts.

Who it's shared with

  • Host colleges: once results are published, the public attendee list shows attendees' names (and any group "party" name) — never your email, PIN, or dietary requirements. Your dietary requirements are shared only with the organiser and the host college for catering, via admin-only rosters.
  • Email delivery: we send email through Resend; your email address and message contents pass through them to be delivered.
  • Chatbot (if enabled): the assistant is powered by Anthropic's API. It is only sent public information about formals — never your email, PIN, dietary details, or contact details.
  • We do not sell your data or use third-party advertising or tracking.

Cookies

We use a single, strictly-necessary session cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) to keep you logged in and protect forms against CSRF. There is no advertising or analytics tracking, so no cookie banner is needed.

Your rights

  • Access / portability: download everything we hold about you as JSON from My formals → “Your data & privacy”.
  • Erasure: delete your account and personal data at any time from the same place. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Rectification: update your details by re-registering with the same email, or ask an admin.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is erased immediately; a minimal, non-identifying audit entry (a numeric id and the action) may be retained for security and integrity. Database backups are kept for up to 7 days and then rotated out.
